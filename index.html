ABOUT THE ACADEMY

Education that shapes the whole person.

At Faith Academy Eket, learning is about more than the classroom. It is about nurturing thoughtful, capable young people who can serve their communities with confidence and character.

Our school-related public information describes a Christian, co-educational secondary-school community that values spirituality, leadership, honesty, integrity, industry and purposeful living.

Explore our values →

We believe strong learning grows alongside discipline, creativity, compassion and a sense of responsibility.
